# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical trade-offs.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus on the issue, not the person.
- Protect private information and credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Threats, sexualized language, or unwelcome contact.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or infrastructure.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
